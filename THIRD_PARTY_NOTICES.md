# Third-party assets

What this project uses that it did not make, where it came from, and whether it is in this
repository. Nothing here may be committed unless its licence allows redistribution.

| Asset | Used for | Licence | In the repository? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blaster Kit 2.1** by Kenney ([kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit)) | The Blaster Pistol sidearm model (`blaster-a`) and its palette texture, in `Assets/Game/Conquest/Art/Weapons/KenneyBlasterKit/` | CC0 1.0 (public domain); crediting Kenney is welcome but not required. The kit's own `License.txt` is kept next to the model. | Yes (CC0 allows redistribution) |
| **Free Sound Effects Pack** (Unity Asset Store, product 155776) | Primary and sidearm shots, explosions, overheat/vent, grenade throw, station deploy and surface impact sounds | Unity Asset Store EULA: usable in builds, but the raw files may not be published | **No.** `Assets/Free SFX Pack/` is in `.gitignore`. See README, "Sound effects". |
| Unity template and sample content (`Assets/Templates/`, `Assets/Audio/Clips/Original FPSSample Clips/`, ParticlePack) | Rifle model, character rig, particle materials and textures, fallback and UI sounds | Unity Companion License / Unity sample terms | Yes (as imported from Unity's own templates) |

Fonts, packages and the engine come from Unity's package registry and are not listed here.
