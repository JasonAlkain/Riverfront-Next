# Third-party assets

What this project uses that it did not make, where it came from, and whether it is in this
repository. Nothing here may be committed unless its licence allows redistribution.

| Asset | Used for | Licence | In the repository? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blaster Kit 2.1** by Kenney ([kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit)) | The Heavy and Recon primary models and their palette texture: `blaster-p` (Heavy), `blaster-f` (Recon), in `Assets/Game/Conquest/Art/Weapons/KenneyBlasterKit/` | CC0 1.0 (public domain); crediting Kenney is welcome but not required. The kit's own `License.txt` is kept next to the model. | Yes (CC0 allows redistribution) |
| Unity template and sample content (Multiplayer Building Blocks, the Multiplayer FPS sample and its ParticlePack), now in `Assets/Game/Shared/` | Rifle, handgun and shotgun models (`Weapons/`), character rig (`Characters/Armature/`), particle materials and textures (`FX/ParticlePack/`), weapon, explosion, footstep and UI sounds (`Audio/`) | Unity Companion License / Unity sample terms | Yes (as imported from Unity's own templates) |

Fonts, packages and the engine come from Unity's package registry and are not listed here.
